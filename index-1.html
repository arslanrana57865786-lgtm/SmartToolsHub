Image Compressor, Resizer & Converter

Reduce file size, change dimensions or convert format. Supports JPG, PNG and WebP.

Limits: PNG output is lossless, so quality and target size apply to JPG/WebP. Animated GIFs become still images.
Advertisement

How to compress an image online

Add your images, choose JPG or WebP, lower the quality or enter a target size in KB, then press Process. Compare before and after sizes and download each result. Resizing to a smaller width is the most effective way to shrink photos for email and forms.

PDF Optimizer & Merger

Clean up and re-pack PDFs, or merge several into one.

Honest limits: this is lossless optimization (strips metadata, compacts structure). It will not re-compress embedded images, so scanned PDFs may shrink only a little. Encrypted PDFs are not supported.

Passport Photo Studio

Replace a plain background with a standard colour and export at passport size.

Works best on a plain, evenly lit background. It removes colours similar to the photo edges; busy backgrounds or clothing matching the background may need a lower sensitivity. Check official photo rules before submitting.
